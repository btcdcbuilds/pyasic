# Player feedback for the playtest agent

Write your notes from playing on your phone here, newest first. The playtest agent reads this file
before every playtest and checks each point in the game.

## 2026-10-24 (v1.0.20)

- Pretty cool overall.
- At some stages the guns were so powerful I couldn't see the signs: I couldn't see the gate numbers going up.
- Need an information part that explains the different things: e.g. a red gate (the count changes faster when you shoot it), what a red ÷2 does, etc.
- The game is a little slow to play.
- In our game it feels like we're driving through the level. In the other games everything comes to you: hundreds of enemies that are always there and you have to keep at bay, and the same for weapons and people.

## 2026-10-11 (v1.0.17)

- The Bitcoin theme isn't really a Bitcoin theme: it's mostly orange. Put the Bitcoin logo and more Bitcoin things in it.
- Enemies die on the first bullet. There should be loads of them and they should disappear one by one as you shoot.
- The last big guy (boss) sits in a bubble. Bullets should start killing him from as far back as possible.
- When we go through the banners (people, coins, weapons), the enemy behind them isn't getting shot.
- The part where the enemy comes at us and we shoot them needs a lot of work.

## 2026-10-11 (v1.0.3)

- The enemy needs a lot of work. It doesn't work properly.
- The end fight against the big guy (boss) is not very thoughtful.
- There should be more to the level after killing the first big guy (the level shouldn't just end there).
- Credits, people and rewards simply time out, and some have a max amount. That's stupid.
- Bullets still don't make it all the way up the track.

## 2026-10-10 (v1.0.2)

- Not very fluent (smoothness).
- Bullets don't go far enough.
- No real anxiety when the attackers are coming.
- It's weird that I can hide from attackers in a walled-off lane.

## 2026-10-10 (v1.0.1)

- Too blocky, especially the kills and effects.
- Levels are too short.
- Rewards are captured even when I'm in the wrong lane.
- Shooting comes in bursts about once a second instead of a constant stream.
