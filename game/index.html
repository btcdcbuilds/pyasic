How to play

Controls

Drag left and right to steer. Your army shoots on its own. The bigger your army, the more bullets fly.

Gates: run through one

+12

Adds soldiers. Shoot it before you reach it and the number goes up. Each extra point takes a few more hits than the last.

-8

Takes soldiers away. Shoot it: every 2 hits knock 1 off, and at 0 it flips to a blue + gate. A flipped gate grows back to its old size quickly (2 hits a point), so red gates are worth shooting!

x2

Doubles your army. Shooting doesn't change it.

÷2

Halves your army. Shooting doesn't change it. Steer around it!

+15%DAMAGE

Every soldier hits harder (or shoots faster with FIRE RATE) for the rest of the level. Shooting doesn't change it.

Things to shoot open

40

Weapon crate. Get in its lane and shoot the number to 0. It turns gold (RUN IN!) and runs to you: your whole army gets that gun. The same gun again gives +15% damage.

HERO60

Hero crate. Same, but a minigun hero joins and fights beside your army.

FREE +1230

Prison cage. Shoot it to 0 and the prisoners join you.

45

Wall. Shoot it down or go around. Running into a crate, cage or wall that still has a number costs you soldiers.

TNT

Red barrel. Shoot it to blow up enemies and walls nearby. Running into it costs 6 soldiers.

Pick up

+1

Coins. Run through them. Spend coins on weapons and upgrades.

Enemies and dangers

27

Enemy squad. The number is how many are left. They charge at you and shoot back, so mow them down before they reach you. Each enemy that reaches you takes some of your soldiers with it.

!

Saw blades, rolling boulders and lava geysers cost soldiers every time they hit. Watch their timing and steer around.

| | |

Walled lanes. Once inside you can't switch lanes, but enemies jump the low walls. Pick your lane before you go in.

Bosses

Each level has 2 or 3 stages and every stage ends with a boss. Start shooting it from far away. When it attacks, a red zone appears on the ground: steer out before it lands. After an attack its chest glows: hit the weak spot for double damage. Beating a mid-boss sends reinforcements.

Bonus run and stars

After the final boss, march up the bonus steps. Each step costs soldiers and raises your coin multiplier (up to x5). Stars depend on how many soldiers you finish with.
