# Privacy Policy: Army Rush: Worlds at War

_Last updated: 2026-10-11_

Army Rush: Worlds at War ("the game") is an offline game.

**What we collect:** nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it does not make network requests.

**What is stored on your device:** your game progress and settings (levels cleared, coins, upgrades, stars, sound and vibration preferences, play statistics). These are kept only in the game's private storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere.

**Deleting your data:** open Settings → Reset all progress in the game, or uninstall the game. Either one removes everything it stored.

**Permissions:** the game may vibrate your phone for feedback (you can switch this off in Settings), and it keeps the screen awake while you play. The Android build also declares the standard internet permission that its app framework includes by default. The game itself does not use the internet.

**Children:** the game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

**Changes:** if this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.

**Contact:** see the developer contact email on the game's store page.
