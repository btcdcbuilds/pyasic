# 08 — Legal, Tax & Accounting

> This is a checklist for briefing a Thai lawyer and accountant, **not legal advice**.
> Items marked "verify" must be confirmed by them. The goal is "properly covered, not over the top".

## 1. Company setup

| Item | Notes | Status |
|------|-------|--------|
| Entity | Thai private limited company (บริษัทจำกัด) registered with the DBD | To do |
| Shareholding | If any foreign shareholder: retail and most services are on **Foreign Business Act List 3**. A foreign stake must be under 50% unless an FBL is granted or the capital thresholds are met (retail: total capital ≥ THB 100M, or ≥ THB 20M per store, per [BOI OSOS FAQ](https://osos.boi.go.th/C/faq-group/26/Minimum-capital-for-Retail-sale-of-goods)) | Verify current rules with lawyer |
| Nominee warning | Thai "nominee" shareholders holding shares on a foreigner's behalf is illegal under the FBA. Use a genuine structure | Lawyer |
| Work permit | Any foreigner working in the business needs a visa + work permit. Commonly cited requirements: paid-up capital per foreign employee and Thai staff ratios | Verify with lawyer |
| VAT registration | Required once annual turnover exceeds THB 1.8M (verify). Register **before** the first import so import VAT can be reclaimed | Accountant |
| Customs registration | Register as an importer with Thai Customs (e-Customs / paperless) | Broker |
| E-commerce | Since June 2024 companies registered under the Civil & Commercial Code were reported as exempt from separate e-commerce commercial registration ([Legal500](https://legal500.com/developments/thought-leadership/thailand-updates-e-commerce-regulations-what-you-need-to-know)). Check whether DBD "Registered" trust mark and OCPB direct-marketing registration apply | Verify |
| Bank account | Corporate account; PromptPay for the company | To do |
| Trademark | Register the brand name + logo with the Department of Intellectual Property | To do |

## 2. Licences & permits

| Licence | Needed for | Authority | Status |
|---------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| TISI import permit / certified products | Controlled electrical parts in the units (cables confirmed controlled; others to check) | TISI | Verify |
| Goods-transport operating licence | Truck hire for other people's goods | Department of Land Transport | Verify before launching truck hire |
| Signboard tax | Warehouse signage | Local authority | Accountant |
| Building/land-use for warehouse | Our premises | Local authority | Lawyer, as part of lease review |
| Electrical work | Connecting units to supply | Licensed electrician (skills certificate) | Use licensed staff/contractors |
| Engineering sign-off | Pile designs, stacked accommodation | Licensed engineer (Council of Engineers) | Engage an engineer |

## 3. Customer contracts & documents

| Document | Key clauses |
|----------|-------------|
| **Terms of sale** | What's included/excluded; prices ex/inc VAT; quote validity; deposit, balance and when ownership passes (retention of title until fully paid); delivery terms; cancellation and deposit refund rules; force majeure; Thai law + venue |
| **Permit & site responsibility clause** | The buyer is responsible for building permits, land rights, site access and local rules; we provide standard drawings/specs to help their application. No promise that a permit isn't needed |
| **Delivery & installation terms** | Site access requirements (road width, overhead lines, ground conditions); extra charges for failed delivery; buyer signs the site checklist |
| **Warranty** | Separate terms for structure, roof/leaks, panels, electrics, piles; what voids it (modifications, unlicensed electrical work, moving the unit without us); claim process via the support channel. Back it up with the factory warranty |
| **Electrical safety notice** | Connection to mains must be done by a licensed electrician; RCD protection; no DIY changes to the switchboard |
| **Handover form** | Unit serial, condition photos, tests done, customer signature: starts the warranty |
| **Truck hire job sheet** | Load description and weight, customer declares the contents, liability limits, insurance excess, cancellation |
| **Reservation / pre-order agreement** | For pre-selling the first container: deposit amount, refund conditions if shipment is delayed, price lock |
| **Supplier agreement** | Specs/drawings as attachments, quality standard, inspection rights, defect remedy, warranty back-to-back with ours, delivery penalties, Form E obligation, IP/branding |
| **Rental agreement (phase 2)** | Term, rent, damage, relocation, insurance, return condition |

## 4. Product liability & consumer protection

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Unsafe goods liability | Thailand's product-liability law (Liability for Injuries from Unsafe Goods Act B.E. 2551) can make **importers and sellers** liable for unsafe products, not only the manufacturer. Verify scope with the lawyer |
| Product liability insurance | Get a quote. This is the main reason to insure |
| Consumer protection (OCPB) | Advertising claims must be accurate; Thai-language labels/manuals may be required for some goods. Check with the lawyer |
| Direct marketing | If we sell directly to consumers online, check whether OCPB direct-marketing registration applies |

## 5. Data protection (PDPA)

| Requirement | How we meet it |
|-------------|----------------|
| Privacy notice | On the website, in the first bot message (link) and on forms |
| Marketing consent | Separate, not pre-ticked; logged with timestamp + channel ([Tilleke guidance summary](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/thailand-releases-new-draft-pdpa-guidance-on-lawful-bases-and-marketing/)) |
| Opt-out | "STOP"/"ยกเลิก" keyword on all channels; agents honour it immediately |
| Data subject requests | Routed to a human; respond within the legal deadline (verify) |
| Data processors | Agreements with the cloud/AI/messaging providers; cross-border transfer basis (verify) |
| Retention | Keep tax records as legally required; delete marketing data on withdrawal |
| Security | Access control, encryption, audit logs |

## 7. Tax & accounting

| Item | Detail | Who |
|------|--------|-----|
| VAT | 7% (extended to 30 Sep 2027 by Royal Decree 807: [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/th/en/insights/doing-business-in-thailand/tax/thailand-maintains-7-vat-rate-for-another-year)). Import VAT paid at customs is input tax; monthly VAT return (ภ.พ.30) | Accountant |
| Tax invoices | Full tax invoices for B2B buyers (they need them to claim VAT); e-Tax invoice optional | Admin agent drafts, accountant/system issues |
| Withholding tax | On services we pay for (e.g. contractors, rent, transport) and that B2B customers withhold from our service fees (install, delivery, truck hire). Rates vary by type: accountant to set up | Accountant |
| Corporate income tax | Standard rate 20%, with reduced SME rates if eligible (verify current thresholds) | Accountant |
| Customs duty | Cost of goods; keep all import declarations for audit | Broker + accountant |
| Bookkeeping | Monthly; audited annual financial statements (a licensed auditor is required for companies) | Accounting firm |
| Inventory | Track by unit serial number from PO to delivery; landed cost per unit (doc 09) | System |
| Payroll | Social security, personal income tax withholding (ภ.ง.ด.1) | Accountant |
