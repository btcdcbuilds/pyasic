## 2. Flatbed truck (two jobs: our deliveries + hire)

| Decision | Recommendation | Why |
|----------|----------------|-----|
| Truck type | Flatbed with a **truck-mounted crane (HIAB-type)** | Lets us unload and place units on screw piles without hiring a separate crane on every job |
| Size | Long enough for our largest folded unit; payload above the heaviest unit + piles + tools | Get folded dimensions and weight from the supplier first |
| New vs used | Used to start (cash), with a full inspection | |
| Driver licence | Thai truck licence class matching the truck's weight; crane-operation training | Confirm class with the Department of Land Transport (DLT) |
| Hire-and-reward licence | Carrying other people's goods for money likely needs a goods-transport operating licence from the **DLT** under the Land Transport Act B.E. 2522 | ❗ Confirm with DLT/lawyer **before** advertising truck hire. Our own deliveries (our goods) may be treated differently |
| Insurance | Compulsory (Por Ror Bor) + commercial voluntary insurance + goods-in-transit + crane liability | Broker to quote |
| Tracking | GPS tracker | Shows live ETA to customers via the agents; also commonly required for commercial trucks (confirm) |

### Truck hire as its own line

## 3. Delivery & installation process

| Step | Who / what | Output |
|------|-----------|--------|
| 1. Site survey | Customer sends photos + location pin via LINE/WhatsApp (agent requests specific photos: access road, ground, overhead wires, slope); site visit for big jobs | Site checklist in CRM |
| 2. Engineering | Pile layout per unit size (standard drawings signed by a licensed engineer); custom for stacking or poor soil | Pile plan |
| 3. Schedule | Dispatch agent proposes dates; customer confirms | Calendar booking |
| 4. Pile installation | Crew installs and levels the piles | Photos with level readings |
| 5. Delivery + placement | Truck + crane places the unit on the piles; unit fixed to the pile heads | Photos |
| 6. Unfold + seal | Crew unfolds, seals joints, tests doors/windows | Checklist |
| 7. Electrical | Unit's external inlet connected to supply **by a licensed electrician** (ours or the customer's) | Test record (insulation, RCD trip test) |
| 8. AC (if ordered) | Partner installer | Commissioning record |
| 9. Handover | Customer walkthrough; signs handover; warranty registered; manual + QR code to the support chat | Signed handover form |
| 10. After-sales | Support agent check-ins at 7 days, 30 days, 6 months | CSAT, reviews, referrals |
