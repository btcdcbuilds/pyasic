# 12 — Business Name Shortlist

Before choosing: check each name (1) with the DBD company-name reservation
system, (2) in the Department of Intellectual Property trademark search, (3)
for `.co.th` / `.com` domain availability, and (4) on Facebook, LINE OA, Lazada
and TikTok handles. **None of these checks have been done yet.**

| # | Name | Thai version | Meaning / angle | Notes |
|---|------|--------------|-----------------|-------|
| 1 | **BaanPub** | บ้านพับ | "Folding house" in Thai: says exactly what it is | Easy for Thai buyers to search and remember |
| 2 | **FoldSpace** | โฟลด์สเปซ | Fold-out space for any use | Works for offices, homes and accommodation |
| 3 | **SiteBox Thailand** | ไซต์บ็อกซ์ | Site office in a box | Strong for construction / data-centre buyers |
| 4 | **ReadyRoom** | เรดี้รูม | A room that arrives ready | Good for homeowners |
| 5 | **UnfoldHub** | อันโฟลด์ฮับ | Unfold and use | Modern / tech feel |
| 6 | **PortPod** | พอร์ตพอด | Pods from the port, ready now | Fits a stock-near-Laem-Chabang story |
| 7 | **SnapOffice** | สแนปออฟฟิศ | Office set up in a snap | Office-focused. Less fit for homes |
| 8 | **PodPlus Thailand** | พอดพลัส | The pod plus everything (piles, furniture, AC, delivery) | Matches the one-stop offer |
| 9 | **QuickBaan** | ควิกบ้าน | Quick house (English + Thai) | Homeowner-friendly |
| 10 | **SiteNest** | ไซต์เนสต์ | A home base on any site | Works for staff accommodation |

**Recommendation:** *BaanPub* for Thai consumer search, or *SiteBox Thailand*
for B2B credibility. The truck line can run as a sub-brand (e.g. "SiteBox Truck & Crane").
